## 1. Verdict

[inferred] CP1--CP5 and the post-CP5 H1 intervention-aware C-sweep follow-up are now closed. CP5 completed the n=200 FaithEval H-neuron/control bundle and passed the artifact-contract/prompt/parser checks, but it **did not pass** the Mistral H-neuron intervention-replication gate. The H1 follow-up selected a different coupled-score classifier (`C=0.75`, 9 positive H-neurons) and still produced a flat FaithEval curve. Do not write that Mistral replicated the H-neuron intervention effect. [inferred] Bridge remains the cleanest Gemma anchor; any Mistral bridge/ITI work after this point must be framed as a separate transfer audit, not as the next step after a successful Mistral H-neuron gate.

[inferred] The immediate next step is **not** another classifier, another local C-grid tweak, a split-stability rerun, another CP4/CP5 rerun, or a Mistral SAE. CP3 gives a held-out sparse readout, CP4 proves the FaithEval prompt/parser/control plumbing is comparable, CP5 shows that the AUROC-selected readout does not translate into a claim-bearing FaithEval intervention effect, and H1 shows that the paper-style coupled C criterion does not rescue that effect on Mistral 2501. The CP5 control summary's raw `specificity_supported` triage is rejected under the stricter acceptance rule because the paired endpoint effects are null and the H/control alpha-0 baseline offset already explains the endpoint offset.

[observed/inferred] 2026-05-06 anchor-3 jailbreak update: the Mistral JBB harmful500 measurement branch is completed and reviewed. It shows a strong full-output safety-surface curve across binary, CSV-v3, and StrongREJECT judges, with all four evaluators valid for 2000/2000 rows and endpoint effects around +31 to +33 pp from alpha 0.0 to 3.0. This is measurement evidence and a useful CSV-v3/StrongREJECT same-output comparison; it is **not** a Mistral H-neuron specificity claim because this branch lacks matched random-neuron/layer-matched controls. Use the 2026-05-06 report as the single source for all anchor-3 jailbreak numbers.

[inferred] SAE decision: **drop from the Mistral critical path**. Do not spend claim-bearing GPU on a Mistral SAE on the assumption that CP5 succeeded; it did not. No exact public Gemma-Scope-2-equivalent exists for `mistralai/Mistral-Small-24B-Instruct-2501`; the visible Mistral-family SAE candidate is a single-layer different-checkpoint artifact, not a matched substitute. [inferred] Make the SAE comparison explicitly Gemma-only unless a new, pre-registered Mistral branch is justified as exploratory/null-classification work.

[speculative] Biggest risk is not GPU cost; it is measurement confounding from tokenization, chat template, prompt style, and judge drift. [inferred] After CP5, the paper cannot claim that the H-neuron control gate is non-Gemma-idiosyncratic. Any future Mistral ITI/bridge result would address a different transfer question and should not be used to imply that the H-neuron intervention gate passed.
